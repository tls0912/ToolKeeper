汗青 - Markdown Writer brings your documents into focus. Open local Markdown files in a readable preview, then switch to editing when you need to make changes. Use it for README files, notes, and written guides.

In reading mode, a chapter list on the left gathers H1–H6 headings so you can jump to a section. In editing mode, write Markdown on the left and see a live preview on the right. Keep several documents open in tabs, and use search and replace to revise your text.

Bamboo Light and Bamboo Dark themes pair a bamboo interface with a xuan paper background. Set fonts and sizes separately for the interface, preview, and editor. Choose traditional lettering or fonts installed on your computer. The editing toolbar also helps you insert headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, and links.

Work with tables, task lists, code blocks, footnotes, and local images. To share a finished document, save the Markdown source and export a timestamped PDF beside it. Autosave, external change notifications, and draft recovery after a crash support your everyday work.

Document processing and preview happen locally. The interface is available in Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese.

Try it free for 7 days. Microsoft Store manages the trial period and license. After the trial expires, a one-time purchase of the full version is required to continue using the app.

Preview and PDF export require Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.
