Version 1.0.3
Adds Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Arabic, French, and Korean, bringing the interface to eight languages.
Shows three built-in reading articles in turn when no document is open, each available in all eight languages.
Adds an option to remember open files when closing the app and reopen named files on the next launch.
Applies the selected H1–H6 outline levels to both the preview chapter list and PDF bookmarks.
Refines recent files, taskbar titles, the font menu, and startup initialization.
