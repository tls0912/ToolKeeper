汗青 - Markdown Writer Privacy Policy
Updated: October 4, 2026
Publisher: 不告訴你

1. Documents and local processing
Hanqing reads Markdown documents you choose to open, associated file paths, and permitted local images referenced by those documents for editing, preview, saving, and PDF export. This content may include personal information you put into your documents. The app has no feature that uploads documents or PDFs to a publisher server and does not require a Hanqing account.

2. Data stored on your device
The app stores preferences such as language, theme, and fonts, recent file paths, recovery drafts of unsaved documents, error logs, local WebView2 data, and a record of a confirmed full license in the current Windows user's application data area. If you enable remembering open files, it also stores the paths of named files and the active file to reopen on the next launch; disabling this option clears that list. The built-in article rotation position is also stored locally. Drafts may contain full document text; error logs may contain file paths and exception details. Hanqing does not automatically send these files to the publisher. Recovery drafts are still created when automatic saving is disabled. Recent files are limited to 20 paths; error logs rotate without a fixed retention period. Other local data may remain across sessions until the app cleans it up or you delete it.

3. Microsoft services
The Store edition checks Microsoft Store licensing and reads whether the license is active, whether it is a trial, and expiration information. After a full purchase is confirmed, a local confirmation record protected for the current Windows user is saved for subsequent launches. Microsoft handles account sign-in, purchases, and payment. Hanqing does not request or store your Microsoft account password or credit card details. Preview and PDF export use Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. Microsoft Store, Windows, and WebView2 service and diagnostic data practices are covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

4. Links, networking, and sharing
Document preview blocks remote images and network requests initiated by document content. Clicking a web link opens your default browser; the destination website may process information under its own policy. Copying text, links, or email addresses uses the Windows clipboard; clipboard history and synchronization depend on your Windows settings. Choosing to visit the Store opens this product's Microsoft Store page. If you save documents to a network location or cloud-synchronized folder, that service may separately process the files. The app has no built-in advertising or usage analytics tracking.

5. Your control and data security
You can view, edit, or delete your saved documents and exported PDFs, and change preferences and automatic saving in the app. Resetting settings resets preferences, recent files, the remembered open-file list, and the article rotation position. It does not delete documents, drafts, logs, WebView2 data, or the license record. After closing the app, you can manage or delete Hanqing's own local preferences, drafts, logs, WebView2 data, and license record. Deleting drafts removes their recovery content; deleting the license record requires a new license check on a subsequent launch. Hanqing does not add encryption to documents, preferences, drafts, or logs; use appropriate Windows access controls and device protection. The publisher cannot remotely access or delete files on your computer through Hanqing.

6. Policy updates
This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
